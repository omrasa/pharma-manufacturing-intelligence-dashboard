CAPA Guidance for Pharma Manufacturing

High Temperature:
Possible root causes include reactor cooling instability, heat exchanger inefficiency, incorrect temperature setpoint, or temperature sensor calibration drift.
Recommended CAPA actions include inspecting the cooling loop, verifying temperature sensor calibration, reviewing heat transfer efficiency, and checking reactor control settings.

Low Yield:
Possible root causes include raw material variability, incomplete reaction conversion, product loss during transfer, purification inefficiency, or incorrect process parameters.
Recommended CAPA actions include reviewing supplier COA, checking batch records, investigating transfer losses, evaluating purification performance, and confirming reaction completion criteria.

High Pressure:
Possible root causes include pressure control valve malfunction, downstream blockage, pump instability, gas flow restriction, or incorrect pressure setpoint.
Recommended CAPA actions include inspecting the pressure control valve, checking downstream flow restrictions, verifying pump operation, reviewing pressure sensor calibration, and confirming pressure control settings.

Batch Release:
Batches with high temperature and low yield should be held for QA investigation.
Batches with low yield or high pressure should require QA review before release.
Batches with stable temperature, pressure, and yield above target may proceed to QA approval.

Deviation Handling:
Critical deviations require immediate QA notification, deviation record creation, root-cause investigation, CAPA assignment, effectiveness check, and documented closure.
High severity deviations require investigation before release.
Medium severity deviations require monitoring and documentation.
