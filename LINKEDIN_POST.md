# LinkedIn Post

I built an AI-assisted Pharma Manufacturing Intelligence Platform as a portfolio project to connect my pharma/scientific background with data, AI, and digital manufacturing.

The platform includes:

- Batch monitoring
- Deviation detection
- QA/CAPA support
- AI root cause investigation
- RAG-based QA assistant
- Batch genealogy and traceability
- Predictive failure detection
- Process optimization
- Equipment monitoring
- Audit trail
- Enterprise login
- Manufacturing copilot

Tech stack:

Python, Streamlit, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Pandas, Plotly, OpenAI API, LangChain, ChromaDB, ReportLab, and Docker.

The goal was to simulate how pharma manufacturing teams can use data and AI to support quality decisions, process understanding, CAPA workflows, and GMP-style traceability.

This project helped me strengthen my practical skills in:

- Data-driven manufacturing
- AI-assisted QA workflows
- Pharma digitalization
- Full-stack product thinking
- Process intelligence

I am especially interested in opportunities where pharma, manufacturing, data, and AI meet.
